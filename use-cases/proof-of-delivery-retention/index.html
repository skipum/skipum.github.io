Use case 03 / 08

Carriers retain proof of delivery for 90–120 days. Your auditors, customers, and disputes don't. IL retains every POD, signature, and tracking event indefinitely — alongside the brokerage and supporting documents that prove the chain of custody.

What IL helps you do

  • Instantly retrieve PODs for disputes or audits, regardless of age
  • Upload brokerage or supporting documents directly to shipments
  • Associate documents at the shipment, order, or PO level
  • Search across years of historical shipments

Business impact

  • Reduce claims resolution time from days to minutes
  • Support long-term compliance and audits
  • Eliminate manual document searches and back-and-forth with carriers

Why teams adopt this first.

Customers in regulated industries — medical devices, financial services, electrical distribution — tell us POD retention alone justifies the platform. The first time you pull a two-year-old signature in 30 seconds during an audit, you stop thinking about the 90-day cliff.
