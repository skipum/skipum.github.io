Exceptions monitoring.

Proactively identify and resolve shipping issues before they escalate.

Schedule a demo All use cases
Use case 06 / 08

Exceptions live in dozens of carrier portals, each with its own taxonomy. IL normalizes them into one queue — categorized, filterable, and drillable down to the affected shipment.

What IL helps you do

  • Real-time exception identification across parcel, LTL, and freight
  • Categorized exception types (delays, holds, delivery failures, missing scans, customs)
  • Exception trends by carrier, service, lane, and location
  • Drill-down to affected shipments for immediate investigation
  • Fully customizable and filterable views per role and team

Business impact

  • Faster issue resolution
  • Improved service levels
  • Reduced downstream customer escalations

Why teams adopt this first.

Exception monitoring is where IL most often replaces a homegrown spreadsheet workflow. One queue, one taxonomy, one source of truth — across every carrier the business uses.
