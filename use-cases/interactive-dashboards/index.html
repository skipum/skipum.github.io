Use case 08 / 08

IL ships with role-based dashboards for ops, finance, and leadership — and lets every user customize their own. Volume, carrier mix, exceptions, spend, and service performance, in one view, drillable to the shipment.

What IL helps you do

  • Monitor volume, carrier mix, and exceptions in one view
  • Drill from network-level KPIs down to individual shipments
  • Individual customization of each dashboard
  • Share live views with operations, finance, and leadership

Business impact

  • Faster, data-driven operational decisions
  • A single source of truth across logistics stakeholders
  • Less time building reports, more time acting on insight

Why teams adopt this first.

Customers describe IL as "the dashboard the executive team finally agrees on." When ops, finance, and leadership all start a meeting from the same view, the conversation moves from "what does the data say?" to "what are we going to do?"
